Whether the view is behind the shoulder or at the eyes on foot. The key that switches the camera sets it, and a vehicle keeps a choice of its own.
Whether the view is behind the vehicle or at the eyes while seated in one. The key that switches the camera sets it while seated.
How much the camera moves on its own: the drift of a camera held by hand, the bob of the steps and the lean into turns. Lower it if the motion makes you feel unwell.
How much the camera shakes when you are hit, bitten or land hard, and with explosions and thunder near, in first and third person.
aHow much a shot kicks the camera, in first and third person. Where the shot goes does not change.
oHow much a melee swing moves the camera, and how hard a blow jolts it when it lands, in first and third person.
How far behind the character the camera stands, in every state. The character keeps its size on the screen whatever the third-person field of view.
How far above its usual height the camera stands, in every state (squares; below 0, lower). The camera still looks where you look, so the character sits lower on the screen.
RThe same while aiming: the camera moves between the two as aiming starts and ends.
How much further out over the shoulder the camera stands (squares; below 0, nearer the middle). It follows the shoulder when the camera swaps.
In a vehicle: how far behind it the camera stands. The vehicle keeps its size on the screen whatever the third-person field of view.
IIn a vehicle: how far above its usual height the camera stands (squares).
cIn a vehicle: how far to the right of the middle the camera stands (squares; below 0, to the left).
NWhich shoulder the camera looks over. The shoulder key swaps it while playing.
nThe camera moves to the other shoulder when a wall is in the way, and back when it clears. Never while aiming.
How fast the view turns while aiming, on top of the zoom's own: 1 keeps a turn the same across the screen however far the view is zoomed in. A pad's stick takes it in first person too.
MBushes and trees between the camera and the character dissolve, as models do.
With the free camera on, draws the third-person camera's rig: the pivot, the shoulder, the camera wanted and the camera placed.
